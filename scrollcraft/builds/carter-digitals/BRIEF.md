# Carter Digitals: Phase 1 creative brief

5 September 2026. Based on the conversation and the five-phase plan the user asked to begin. This records agreed direction and authored implementation choices; it is not a new interview or a claim that the redesign is built.

## Eight brief topics

1. **Vibe.** User: “Premium modern typography and fonts. 3D rendered features and sections. Advanced interactive components and premium copywriting.” Authored interpretation: cinematic, dimensional, confident, personal. Rich black, white, blue, red and selective gold; no purple.
2. **Journey.** The accepted plan: layered hero → selected work → goal selector → transformation → Services Foundry → packages → founder/process/FAQ → project brief. Supporting pages retain their URLs.
3. **Energy.** Authored implementation choice from the accepted plan: a compelling opening, quieter proof and selection, one larger transformation, then a gradual settling toward contact. No repeated spectacle competing for attention.
4. **Emotion and memory.** Curiosity → confidence → recognition → excitement → clarity → reassurance → connection → readiness. The memorable moment is a sample website assembling around the visitor's chosen business goal.
5. **Signature.** User asked for scrolling motion graphics and advanced interactions. The approved plan makes the interaction useful: selecting credibility, enquiries or clarity changes the assembled demonstration and is carried into the brief.
6. **Range.** Premium modern with cinematic depth and editorial hierarchy. Meaningful high-impact visual changes are required. This is not a monochrome minimalist recolour. Rounded geometry and controlled shadows support the genuine 3D scene.
7. **Structure.** Distinct scenes in a guided studio. Native scrolling; one desktop pinned transformation; no continuous flight. This is the structure proposed in the accepted five-phase plan.
8. **Assets.** Existing Carter logo, founder portrait, logo-reveal film, project/prototype captures and Hyperspeed source. Phase 1 inventories them; Phase 2 creates the scene and matching fallback renders. Existing screenshots do not establish paid-client status or verified outcomes.

## Audience and purpose

Pretoria and Gauteng service-business owners comparing a website builder: legal, education, healthcare and other professional services. They need clear work, scope and a route to speak with the person delivering it.

Belief by the end: Carter Digitals understands what my website needs to communicate and can turn it into a clear, credible enquiry journey.

Primary action throughout the redesigned site: **Start a project**. Main destination `/contact#enquiry`; homepage closing form remains directly usable. Secondary action: **View the work**. Phone and WhatsApp stay explicit alternatives.

Tell-someone sentence: **“It's the site where you choose your business goal, watch a website come together around it, and take that direction into your enquiry.”**

Authored silence: the goal selector is intentionally calm before the peak. It is a useful control, not an empty viewport or timed pause. There is no blank pinned dwell anywhere.

## Grammar and fingerprint

Custom grammar: **guided studio**. A short offer-led opening, early work index, one visitor choice, one explanatory transformation, then practical service/scope content and a working brief. Navigation is a compact floating studio menu. The close is a ready-to-complete brief rather than a giant decorative logo.

Constraints: no repeated full-screen pinned sections; no viewport-count quota; no nested cards; no identical three-card grids as the page structure; no full-page WebGL stage; no scrolling required to reach the hero CTA; no hidden semantic content dependent on animation completion.

Fingerprint gate: workspace resolved to `scrollcraft/`; registry is empty on 5 September. There are no previous local rows to compare. Planned dimensions: guided studio; floating work/services/start-project menu; layered website hero; eight scenes about 9.65 desktop viewports; goal-aware brief ending; goal-driven website assembly. Do not append a shipped row until the redesigned experience is actually shipped.

## Phase boundaries

Phase 1 implements form and measurement foundations, CSS tokens, baseline and this design pack. It does not claim the cinematic hero, new navigation, 3D rendering, shared final form or visual QA are completed. Phase 2 builds the signature prototype. Phases 3–5 complete and release the full site.
