# Carter Digitals — Signal Atelier

## Style Prompt

An ultra-premium South African digital studio expressed as a precise signal workshop: architectural space, luminous interface layers, procurement-grade clarity, and a restrained sense of motion. The experience should feel confident and crafted rather than futuristic for its own sake. Depth comes from material contrast, measured perspective, and choreography—not ornamental 3D.

## Colors

- Ink — `#071521`: primary dark canvas and authority
- Porcelain — `#F4F2EB`: warm editorial surface
- Signal blue — `#31B7E7`: interaction, focus, and digital energy
- Oxidised gold — `#C6A15B`: premium detail used sparingly
- Carter coral — `#EF6272`: human warmth and decisive emphasis

## Typography

- Display: Geist Sans at 760–900 for direct, commercial statements
- Editorial voice: Georgia italic, used only for human emphasis
- Utility: Geist Mono for credentials, states, and technical detail

## Motion

- Confident `expo.out` entrances for primary statements
- Deliberate `power3.inOut` scroll-linked depth
- Short, precise hover responses; one ambient motion per focal scene
- Static, fully readable composition when reduced motion is requested

## Signature Experience

The Conversion X-ray is the memorable proof interaction: a tactile before/after lens that lets visitors inspect how positioning, story, experience, and momentum turn a generic website into a premium commercial asset. It should feel like a senior strategist revealing the thinking beneath the pixels, not a cosmetic comparison slider.

The composition uses four material states:

- Obsidian workshop — `#030C13` for concentrated authority
- Porcelain interface — `#F4F2EB` for clarity and editorial restraint
- Oxidised gold — `#D6B46B` for decision points and premium detail
- Signal cyan — `#31B7E7` for engineered energy

## What NOT to Do

- No decorative particle field or cosmetic WebGL scene
- No rainbow gradients or generic purple-neon treatment
- No identical card grids with equal visual weight
- No constant floating on every section
- No motion that delays contact or obscures pricing
